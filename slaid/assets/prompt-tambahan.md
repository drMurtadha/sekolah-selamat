## Tambahan visual mengikut tema

Dihasilkan menggunakan alat image_gen terbina dalam. Semua subjek adalah rekaan.

### keluarga-mendengar.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Editorial photographic illustration for a dignified Malaysian school wellbeing presentation. Fictional Malaysian mother in muted soft mauve baju kurung and simple cream hijab listening warmly to her 15-year-old daughter in white school hijab and turquoise uniform at a small wooden dining table after school. Natural calm conversation, mother and daughter at eye level, school bag and closed workbook nearby, cozy modest Malaysian home, leafy garden through window. Landscape 3:2, medium-wide view, both people framed centrally with generous breathing room. Warm diffused daylight, gentle sage and neutral palette, authentic skin texture, candid unposed moment. Anatomically natural hands. No distress, no violence, no logos, no readable text, not an actual family or event.
