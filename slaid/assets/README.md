# Visual pembentangan

Visual asal ini ialah ilustrasi fotografi yang dijana menggunakan alat image_gen terbina dalam. Tiada murid, guru, sekolah atau kejadian sebenar digambarkan. Label janaan AI disertakan pada slaid.

## sahabat-sekolah.png

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Create a warm editorial photograph for a Malaysian school wellbeing presentation. Fictional diverse Malaysian secondary-school pupils aged 14-16, four friends in modest neat white school uniforms with navy trousers or turquoise pinafore, one girl wearing a white hijab, walking together under a shaded school corridor towards a sunlit tropical school courtyard. Candid natural companionship, relaxed hopeful expressions, not posing for camera. Trees and a modest Malaysian school building. Landscape 3:2 composition, medium-wide eye level, warm soft morning light, subdued forest green, natural skin tones, documentary photographic texture. No distress, no violence, no logos, no readable text. Subjects fully within central framing for crop flexibility. This is an illustrative fictional scene, not an actual incident.

Respectful open conversation, teacher at students eye level, empathetic calm expressions, natural gestures and anatomically correct hands on table. Soft daylight from school library windows, blurred bookshelves, tropical greenery. Landscape 3:2 medium-wide shot with all people centrally composed and generous space, realistic editorial photography, gentle warm neutral tones, no text, no logos, no distress or implied real case.
